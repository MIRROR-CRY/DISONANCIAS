# Mirror Cry: letra (borrador v1)

E minor, 156 BPM, Em-C-Am-B. Referencias: Second Floor, Los Prisioneros, Depeche Mode, música western. Secciones alineadas con `SECTIONS` de `build_song.py`.

## [Intro] (8 compases)
Batería motor a 156 BPM, bajo galopante en corcheas, riff agudo y frío.

## [Verso 1] (8 compases, sin guitarra)
Why should I stay AS-IS?
If I can be TO-BE?
From years ago, the Miss
Taught me the verb To Be

## [Verso 2] (8 compases)
Trapped in an iron cage
Sisyphus wasn't like this
Just knowing what to do
Won't happen any day

## [Puente / Quiebre] (8 compases)
Sin voz. Corte seco de medio compás, silencio. El último compás del puente es el drop silencioso, solo bombo.

## [Coro / Hook] (8 compases, chorus1 y chorus2)
Explosión instrumental completa, apertura estéreo y voz proyectada.

It's a mirror cry driving me
A ghost that won't let me be
I won't lose the opportunity
And that's why I'll miss it

## [Verso 3] (8 compases, sin guitarra; ahora va después del coro)
In front of the perfect girl
Faking a perfect day
No way I won't say hello
Watching me ruin it again

## [Outro] (8 compases, ahora con voz)
I thought I was in front of her
I thought it was the great chance
I look through the glass...

(Corte seco de medio compás, silencio.)

...BUT I'M IN FRONT OF ME AGAIN!
